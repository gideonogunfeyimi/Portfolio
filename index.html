From the classroom to the communications desk

I coordinate marketing and communications at Teach For Nigeria, part of the Teach For All network, where I lead a team of four and work with ten departments. We turn programme evidence and stories from Fellows, alumni and students into publications, campaigns, films and media coverage for donors, partners, policymakers and the public.

I came to this work from the classroom. I taught in a public primary school in Ogun State as a Teach For Nigeria Fellow, then coached 181 Fellows over three years, so I know the schools and people I write about.

I hold an M.Ed. in Educational Administration and Planning from the University of Lagos.

Fundraising

2026 individual giving campaign

₦35 million given by Teach For Nigeria's own community through the 2026 campaigns.

The brief

Teach For Nigeria runs a giving campaign every quarter. In 2026 the aim was to move its own community of board members, staff, Fellows, alumni and friends to give.

What I did

  • Designed and led the communications strategy for the year's giving campaigns.
  • Paused the June campaign to ask team leads why people were not giving, then relaunched it around personal asks. Over nine months the relaunch raised ₦9.8 million and $2,250.
  • Made tailored materials for the board, ambassadors, staff, Fellows and alumni, backed by impact stories.
  • Kept the ask concrete: ₦3 million supports one Fellow for a year, giving 100 children access to quality education.

Campaign

2026 Fellowship recruitment campaign

38,246 people raised their hand, and 622 became Fellows. No paid advertising.

The brief

Recruit Teach For Nigeria's tenth cohort: 550 Fellows and 100 school leaders for public schools in Lagos and Ogun States, without paid advertising.

What I did

  • Led marketing and communications, planning with the recruitment team from December.
  • Ran social media, WhatsApp communities, press outreach and a recruitment jingle side by side, with weekly reporting.
  • Managed communications for the virtual launch, One Vision, Endless Possibilities, streamed live on YouTube.
  • Got staff, Fellows and alumni sharing the materials through their own networks, often on WhatsApp.

The application portal drew 266,068 visits, 2.7 times the target. The campaign was shared as a learning case with Teach For All Africa.
